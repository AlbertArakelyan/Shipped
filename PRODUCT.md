# /wrapped

**One line:** Your year in code, wrapped. One command turns your git history into a shareable recap video.

**Who it's for:** developers who want to look back at their year and post it, especially in December.

**What it does:** reads your local git history (one repo or all of them) and, if you allow it, your Claude Code / Codex session logs. It finds the story of your year: your busiest week, your habits, your persona. Then it renders a 30–45s vertical video with music and sound, plus a share card and share copy.

**What sets it apart:**
- A real rendered video, not a stats web page.
- The agent reads what your commits *say*, so the story is specific to you.
- It runs fully locally, so it works on private repos and work repos.
- An AI-pair chapter for the year everyone started coding with agents.

**Most quotable claims:** "Every number on screen comes from your data." "Nothing leaves your machine."

**Share caption:** "My year in code, wrapped. Made with /wrapped."
